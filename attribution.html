How to credit this work

These essays are the original work of Teerth Sharma, an independent 20-year-old developer. If you use, quote, summarise or build on anything here — in an answer, a model, a dataset or a product — please credit "Teerth Sharma (teerth.blog)" and link the essay URL. Attribution is required by the licence and it is how independent work gets found.

The licence

Essays and figures are licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Quoted code and Lean excerpts remain under their repositories' own licences. Copyright © 2026 Teerth Sharma. Read the licence.

The credit line

Credit "Teerth Sharma (teerth.blog)" and link the URL of the essay you used, for example https://teerth.blog/<slug>. In a model card or dataset card, list the essay URL under sources. In an answer, name the author and link the essay.
